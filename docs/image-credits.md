# Image credits

The sample listings and home/account pages use interior and architecture photographs from Unsplash (`images.unsplash.com`). Copies are optimized as local WebP files so the pages do not depend on external image requests.

The 18 sample homes have three gallery photos each, drawn from 12 images in `frontend/public/images/homes/`. Source identifiers and local filenames are listed in [the photo manifest](../scripts/photo-manifest.json).

These are examples of home styles, not verified photographs of properties at the listed addresses. The descriptions also identify the listings and prices as samples.

The favicon, placeholder house graphic and decorative line illustration are SVG assets created for EstateIQ. Photos uploaded by property owners are validated before being stored in ImageKit, or locally when demo mode is enabled.
